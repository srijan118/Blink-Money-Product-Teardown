# BlinkMoney – Product Teardown

### Trust, Clarity & Activation Review | Fintech Product Case Study

A product teardown of BlinkMoney's Android app and website, exploring how trust, onboarding clarity and activation friction affect the first-time user experience.

**Prepared by:** Srijan Srivastava

## Overview

BlinkMoney aims to help users build long-term wealth without sacrificing liquidity by combining investing, borrowing and compounding into a single financial experience.

I explored the product from a first-time user's perspective to identify friction points that could weaken its core value proposition.

The analysis focused on:
- Trust during financial actions
- Onboarding clarity and activation
- SIP discovery and investment-state visibility
- Borrow discovery and education
- Support responsiveness
- Referral and nominee experiences

## Key Product Findings

| Priority | Finding | Area |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Broken Borrow Journey | UX, Conversion & Trust |
| P0 | SIP State Confusion | UX, Tracking & Engagement |
| P1 | Ambiguous Save CTA | UX & Conversion |
| P1 | Support Responsiveness | Engagement & Trust |
| P1 | Borrow Education Gap | Activation & Conversion |

## Key Recommendations

### 1. Improve Borrow Discovery
- Introduce a trust-first borrow onboarding experience.
- Provide clearer eligibility and borrowing information.
- Explain borrowing against investments through contextual education.
- Add graceful fallback experiences for broken routes.

### 2. Improve SIP State Transparency
- Separate Draft, Active, Incomplete and Failed SIPs.
- Introduce clear progress indicators and mandate status.
- Prevent invalid actions when a scheme is unavailable.

### 3. Improve Saving Onboarding
- Replace ambiguous CTAs with intent-specific labels such as "Start SIP".
- Improve CTA hierarchy and supporting microcopy.
- Introduce beginner-friendly FAQs and trust indicators.

### 4. Strengthen Support Experience
- Add instant support acknowledgement.
- Display expected response times.
- Introduce support tracking and onboarding FAQs.

### 5. Improve Borrow Education
- Explain why users might borrow instead of redeeming investments.
- Add contextual education cards.
- Explore a loan-cost simulator and compounding illustrations.

## Prioritisation Approach

I used a P0–P2 prioritisation framework:

- **P0:** Trust or conversion blockers.
- **P1:** Major onboarding or activation friction.
- **P2:** UX inconsistencies or discoverability issues.

Recommendations were prioritised based on their potential impact on user trust, conversion, engagement and activation.

## Additional Observations

Other issues identified during the exploration included:
- Inconsistent authentication requirements across sessions.
- Referral links and copy-code functionality not working as expected.
- Confusing bank-account empty states.
- Android back-navigation behaviour.
- Nominee setup returning an internal server error.
